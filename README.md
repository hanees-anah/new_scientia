# Scientia India — Digital Product Engineering & Innovation Platform

> **"Engineering Digital Experiences. Building Tomorrow's Technology."**  
> Guided by the core philosophy: *"Products are evolved, not built."*

A modern, high-performance web platform for **Scientia Innovation Private Limited** (`scientiaindia.com`), inspired by top-tier engineering organizations (Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Framer).

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights & Redesign Architecture

1. **Design System & Visual Language**:
   - **Primary**: Deep Midnight Navy (`#101828`)
   - **Accent**: Electric Blue (`#526BFF`)
   - **Supporting**: Soft Lavender (`#E9E9FF`)
   - **Background**: Very Light Slate (`#F7F8FC`)
   - **Typography**: Geometric modern sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Inter*) with high contrast and tight tracking.
   - **Interactive Visuals**: 60fps interactive Canvas particle network with mouse magnetism and ambient glow.

2. **Flagship Products Showcase**:
   - **ParentEye**: Next-gen smart school management ERP and mobile application serving 50,000+ students and parents across 120+ institutions.
   - **Kemedy**: Real-time doctor discovery and digital clinic token scheduling engine, reducing patient waiting room idle time by 65%.
   - **CampXEye**: Higher education and campus administration ERP suite.

3. **Core Engineering Practices**:
   - Product Engineering (Lean MVP to Hyper-Scale)
   - Software Development (Distributed Cloud Systems, Microservices, Resilient APIs)
   - Digital Transformation (Modernizing Legacy Workflows)
   - IoT & Connected Devices (Edge Hardware Ingestion, MQTT, GPS Telemetry)
   - Technology Consulting (Architecture Audits, Scalability Blueprints)
   - Digital Product Innovation (User Friction Elimination, UX/UI Design Systems)

4. **Interactive Features**:
   - **Live Product Experience Simulator**: Tabbed interactive portal for ParentEye, Kemedy, and CampXEye with real-time bus telemetry progress simulation and live clinic queue counter.
   - **Interactive Architecture Scope Estimator**: Dynamic engineering blueprint calculator providing estimated agile timelines and tech stacks.
   - **Enterprise Contact Intake Form**: High-converting consultation intake with validation, timeline choices, and instant interactive feedback.

5. **Multi-Page Architecture (React Router)**:
   - `/` — Homepage (Hero, Philosophy, Services Bento, Products, Approach, Why Scientia, Case Studies, Testimonials, Contact)
   - `/about` — Corporate profile, executive leadership (ex-VP JP Morgan, Sasken, HP), 10-year timeline, dual hubs
   - `/services` — Detailed breakdown of all 6 engineering pillars with deliverables & tech stacks
   - `/products` — Dedicated deep dive into ParentEye, Kemedy, and CampXEye
   - `/work` — Real-world engineering case studies with challenges, solutions, and production metrics
   - `/contact` — Direct channels, Bangalore & Cochin office addresses, inquiry submission

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Scripts

- **Framework**: React 18 + Vite

- **Routing**: React Router DOM (v6)
- **Visuals**: HTML5 Canvas Particle Engine + Canvas Confetti

### Development Commands

